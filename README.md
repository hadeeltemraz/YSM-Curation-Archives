# Atlas of Modular Playstyles — A Curated Model Registry

Welcome to the Atlas of Modular Playstyles, a thoughtfully organized registry that brings together community-created content for sandbox games with deep customization layers. Instead of a simple collection, this repository functions as a living archive—a library where every entry is sorted, tagged, and annotated to help you navigate the vast ecosystem of player-made modifications with confidence and clarity.

Think of it as a well-indexed bookshelf in a grand library: each volume is unique, each author has their own voice, and the Dewey Decimal System here is a carefully crafted tagging scheme that lets you find exactly what inspires your next creative session. Whether you are searching for visual overhauls, gameplay tweaks, or complete thematic conversions, the Atlas ensures that the path from curiosity to installation is smooth, transparent, and rewarding.

## 📚 Why This Atlas Exists

The modding community thrives on passion and ingenuity, but the sheer volume of releases can feel overwhelming. Scattered forums, fragmented social media posts, and outdated wikis often hide the best works beneath layers of noise. This repository solves that problem by acting as a **centralized discovery layer**—a place where quality meets organization, and where every contribution is acknowledged within a logical framework based on authorship and content type.

Our mission is simple: to reduce the time you spend hunting and increase the time you spend enjoying. By maintaining a strict categorization system, we empower both newcomers and veteran players to explore with intention, discovering hidden gems and established classics through a single, navigable interface.

- **Multilingual Interface for Global Reach**: Recognizing the diversity of the community, our documentation and tag descriptions are offered in multiple languages, allowing players from different regions to navigate without linguistic barriers.
- **24/7 Uptime and Support Channel**: We maintain a continuous presence, monitoring the health of the repository and providing prompt assistance to users who encounter issues, ensuring the Atlas remains a reliable resource around the clock.

## 🗂️ Organizational Structure

The Atlas is divided into two primary shelves, each dedicated to a specific type of creative output:

### 1. Visual & Character Models
This section specializes in modifications that alter the appearance and animation of characters and entities. You will find:
- **Character Redesigns**: Reimagined skins, outfits, and proportions.
- **Animation Overrides**: New movement sets, combat stances, and idle behaviors.
- **Accessory Packs**: Additional props, weapons, and wearable items that enhance storytelling.

### 2. Functional & Gameplay Packs
Here, the focus shifts to changing how the game plays. This includes:
- **Pack Expansions**: Add new items, mechanics, or interactive systems.
- **Balance Adjustments**: Fine-tuned difficulty curves and resource rebalancing.
- **Thematic Conversions**: Complete overhauls that transport the base game into new genres or settings.

Each category is further sub-filtered by the original author, enabling you to follow the complete body of work from a single creator—a feature that uncovers progression and stylistic evolution.

## 🔍 Searching and Filtering Like a Pro

The Atlas’s true power lies in its granular filtering system. Here are some advanced techniques to maximize your efficiency:

- **Tag Stacking**: Combine multiple tags (e.g., `fantasy`, `magic`, `armor`) to narrow results to the intersection of those categories. This is far more effective than a single broad term.
- **Author Watchlist**: You can “star” specific authors to receive updates when they publish new content. This curates a personalized feed within the main repository.
- **Version Pinning**: The Atlas records the game version for which each mod is compatible. By pinning your current version, you filter out incompatible entries, preventing frustration and potential save-file corruption.

## 🛠️ Contributing to the Atlas

The richness of this repository depends on the collective effort of its users. If you find a model or pack that deserves a place here, we welcome your contribution.

**How to Submit an Entry:**
1. Verify that the entry does not already exist (search by author and title).
2. Prepare the necessary metadata: author name, resource title, version, and a concise description.
3. Submit a contribution request through the standard pull mechanism. Our curation team will review the metadata for accuracy and assign appropriate tags.

**Why Contribute?**
Every addition strengthens the communal knowledge base. You are not just adding a file; you are building a gateway for future players, preserving the work of talented creators, and fostering a culture of shared appreciation.

## ⚠️ Important Considerations and Disclaimers

While the Atlas is structured and monitored, we must emphasize the nature of user-generated content:

- **Third-Party Content**: All resources listed in this repository are created by independent individuals. The Atlas acts as an index and does not host the original files, rather providing pointers to official sources (unless the author’s repository is mirrored here with explicit consent).
- **Compatibility Risks**: Modifications can affect game performance and stability. Always back up your save files before installation. The Atlas provides compatibility notes, but the ultimate responsibility lies with the user.
- **Respect for Authorship**: We strictly adhere to licensing terms. If you redistribute a work, you must comply with the original author’s license (often MIT for code, but art assets may vary). Our curation ensures that only appropriately licensed content is listed.
- **Dynamic Nature**: Mods are frequently updated or abandoned. An entry that is functional today may change tomorrow. The Atlas does its best to reflect current statuses, but we cannot guarantee perpetual accuracy.
